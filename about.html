OUR SUSTAINABLE PRODUCT RANGE

Made From Natural Sugarcane Bagasse

Our eco-friendly dishes, plates and food containers are made from Bagasse, a natural fibrous material obtained from sugarcane pulp.

After sugarcane is processed, the remaining Bagasse fibre can be transformed into useful food service products instead of being treated as waste.

This provides a practical and sustainable option for restaurants, catering businesses, takeaway services, events and other food service requirements.

  • Made From Sugarcane Bagasse
  • Eco-Friendly & Sustainable
  • Biodegradable & Compostable
  • Suitable For Food Service
  • Strong & Practical Design
