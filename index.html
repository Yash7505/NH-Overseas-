ECO-FRIENDLY BAGASSE PRODUCTS

From Sugarcane To Sustainable Tableware

Our Bagasse dishes, plates and food containers are made from sugarcane pulp, a natural fibrous material left after sugarcane processing.

This material can be transformed into practical food service products, providing an eco-friendly solution for restaurants, catering and takeaway.

  • Eco-Friendly & Sustainable
